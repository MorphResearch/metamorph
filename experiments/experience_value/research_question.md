# Research question and Day-1 boundary

Question: does an execution structure that helps an Agent complete its current
task also help it create experience useful on unseen tasks?

RQ1 compares the within-system retention gains of F and U. RQ2 holds a common
receiver `E*` fixed while it receives `EMPTY`, `M_F`, `M_U`, `RAW_F`, or
`RAW_U`. RQ3, deferred beyond E0, separates acquisition, consolidation, and
use through controlled inputs and local interventions.

E0 establishes only the fairness and traceability of the memory path. Zero,
negative, absent, or unusable artifacts are valid outcomes. A ScriptedAgent
test establishes plumbing, not model learning; no result here supports a claim
that either F or U improves science outcomes.
